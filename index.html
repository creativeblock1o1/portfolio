2) Tiesverse Admin Panel↗ 2) Tiesverse Admin Panel↗

Alongside the main website, I collaborated with my teammates on the design of Tiesverse Foundation’s internal administrative panel and Ties Mail, an email-like communication portal. The admin panel is designed to simplify website content management and organisational updates without requiring changes to the underlying code, while Ties Mail provides a dedicated platform for internal communication.
